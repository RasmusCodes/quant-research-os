# The demo research project

`qros demo --out demo-registry` replays about two months of research (January to March 2025, simulated
clock) on a synthetic market, recording everything in a registry. The public dashboard at
[rasmuscodes.github.io/projects/research-os](https://rasmuscodes.github.io/projects/research-os/) is an
export of this registry. **All data are synthetic**: generated by
[`qros.examples.synthetic_market`](../src/qros/examples/synthetic_market.py) with seed 20261121, chosen
before the demo was first built and never changed. No real securities are involved.

## Why a synthetic market

Because the data-generating process is known, every experiment can be scored against the truth. That
is the test of the validation layer itself: a real effect should survive, noise should not, and each
planted mistake should be caught.

| Planted structure | Truth |
|---|---|
| Market and a futures contract with two-state volatility regimes and Student-t shocks | no predictable mean: timing rules have no edge |
| Quarterly profitability (AR(1)), earning a small daily drift; reports published 30-75 days after quarter end, with a price jump on publication proportional to the surprise | a real, persistent effect; using numbers before publication is look-ahead |
| A slow expected-return component switched off between 2013 and 2016 | momentum is real in-sample and fades |
| A transient price component that mean-reverts within days | short-term reversal is real before costs |
| Delistings after an 80% fall (final return -50%), acquisitions at a premium | a survivor-only universe is biased |
| Splits and quarterly dividends | unadjusted prices contain fake returns |
| Six bad prints in price version 1, corrected in version 2 | data versions matter |

Effect sizes were calibrated on other seeds (101-104) so that the real effects are detectable over the
sample, not overwhelming.

## Reading the results

- The **only robust verdict** is the pre-registered profitability test on the sealed holdout
  (EXP-0065). Its point-in-time siblings on the development windows are *fragile*: their test-window
  significance does not survive the Holm adjustment for the five trials evaluated there, which is why
  a sealed holdout is kept for the final test.
- **Momentum** has deflated Sharpe ratios near 1 in-sample and still fails: the planted effect fades
  in the test window, which the out-of-sample checks catch. Significance in-sample is not robustness.
- The **trend-following sweep** is noise by construction. The best of 45 pairs (in-sample Sharpe 0.38)
  sits above the expected best of 18.8 effectively independent noise trials (0.27), but its deflated
  Sharpe ratio is 0.63, and the family's PBO is 0.70: the in-sample winner lands at or below the
  out-of-sample median in 70% of the splits. Checking the best pairs on the holdout makes looks 4-8
  invalid.
- **Every planted mistake is caught**, and none of the robust verdicts is a false discovery. Real
  effects can still be graded fragile or no-edge: reversal is real before costs but not after, and
  the toy's weak AR(1) dependence is too small to confirm.

## Rebuild

```
qros demo --out demo-registry --report demo-results.md   # about 4-6 minutes
qros --registry demo-registry dashboard --serve          # explore it locally
```

Apart from the deliberately unseeded experiment, the build is deterministic, and its timestamps come
from a simulated clock.
